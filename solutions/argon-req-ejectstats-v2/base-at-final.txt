## Practical significance for merging (added by Claude)

A statistically significant gain is not enough to merge. A version is merged into the SOTA only when
the decisive compare shows **a gain of at least 0.005%**. A change of 20 changed lines or fewer may be
merged from **0.002%**.

Reasons:
- Smaller gains do not move the per-case rank scores.
- Every added mechanism makes the code harder for later agents. The user just had the code cleaned
  up, and that cleanup removed query-memo, whose gain was 0.0012%.

When a version is significant but below this threshold:
- record it in REPORT.md;
- do not stop for a merge;
- either develop the idea further, so that the gain reaches the threshold, or close the item, if an
  oracle or upper bound puts the attainable gain below about 0.01%.
